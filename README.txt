Control your electric floor heating from Homey.

This app connects OJ Microline WiFi thermostats (OWD5 and MWD5) to Homey through the OJ cloud — the same service the official app uses. Thermostats sold under SWATT, Schlüter DITRA-HEAT-E-WiFi and Microtemp are supported too.

What you get
• Set the temperature from Homey, as a temporary comfort override that returns to your schedule, or as a permanent manual hold
• Floor and room temperature, live heating status and the active mode
• Switch between Schedule, Comfort, Manual, Boost, Eco and Frost protection
• Daily energy usage and a running total, plus live power in Homey Energy when you enter the wattage of your heating mat
• Flows: start a boost when you get out of bed, drop to frost protection when everybody leaves, get notified when the heating turns on

Getting started
Make sure your thermostat is connected to WiFi and linked to your account in the OJ Microline app. Then add a device in Homey, choose the app you use and sign in with the same account. All thermostats on the account are added at once.
